About

20 years of growing brands through acquisition.

I'm Matt Burgess. I've spent two decades helping businesses find, convert, and keep customers — and more recently, building the AI tools to do it better.

The short version

I'm a performance marketer who's spent 20 years in the trenches of customer acquisition. I've managed multi-million dollar budgets, built and led marketing teams, and worked across industries from travel and tourism to financial services and comparison.

I started my career in SEO — at Wotif Group, managing organic traffic across some of APAC's largest travel brands. From there I moved into broader digital marketing roles, eventually leading teams that spanned SEO, PPC, content, email, social, and display.

Most recently, I was Head of Marketing at The Motley Fool Australia, where I ran new member acquisition across all channels and built AI-powered marketing systems — compliance checkers, content research tools, copy drafting engines, and workflow automation — from the ground up.

That combination of strategic marketing leadership and hands-on AI system building is what I bring to Conversation Media. The person doing the thinking is the same person doing the building.
